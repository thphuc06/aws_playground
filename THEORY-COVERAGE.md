## Meaning of coverage

- **Explained + exercise:** the cited section supplies an original explanation and a worked scenario, code fragment, review exercise, or reasoning example. Exercises are not necessarily graded questions or executable labs. This does not mean mastery, complete service documentation, or all possible exam questions are covered.
- **Partial:** relevant theory exists but the specified practical detail is still missing; see the row.
- The matrix covers the published numbered **skills**: 101 C02 and 72 C03. Domain knowledge/concept lists are supporting context and are not separately asserted to be exhaustively covered. Named service lists are explicitly nonexhaustive; service presence does not establish skill coverage.
- No AWS account, IAM policy, infrastructure, model invocation, deployment, or real-world integration is executed or verified by these chapters. Browser previews teach bounded slices; theory examples do not add simulator capabilities.
- C03 AI integration examples include scoped retrieval/tool-use illustrations; they do not claim full RAG architecture, foundation-model selection, or complete AI security-framework design as target tasks.
- C03-only chapters are version filtered. Some shared chapters contain explicitly labeled C03 extension sections; common mechanisms remain relevant to both versions. C02 Q Developer references are not silently renamed to Amazon Quick.
- Community repositories were reviewed only as provenance/index candidates; chapters are original official-source synthesis. See LICENSE-RESEARCH.md for license decisions.
